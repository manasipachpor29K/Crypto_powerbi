This project focuses on analyzing and forecasting stock market and cryptocurrency prices using Python and Microsoft Power BI. Historical market data is cleaned, visualized, and analyzed through interactive dashboards showing price trends, trading volume, and company comparisons. Forecasting models like ARIMA, SARIMA, and LSTM are used to predict future prices, where LSTM provided the most accurate results for volatile crypto markets. The project also includes sentiment analysis of financial news to study how positive or negative news affects market prices, helping users make better financial and investment decisions
